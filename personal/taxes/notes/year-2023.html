The corporation

15248132 Canada Inc., operating as XYZ Bytes, was incorporated on 14 August 2023 with Umar as sole director and owner. Its first tax year ran only 140 days, to 31 December.

Lazer Technologies was the only client, paying every two weeks: $82,403.08 including HST, $72,923 before HST. Zeeshan, an offshore subcontractor, was paid $24,955.48 through WISE.

Personal
