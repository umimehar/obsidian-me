A full year with Lazer, MIR joining in October: $238,877 of revenue, $15,848 of tax and $72,550 of dividends declared.

The corporation

Lazer paid all year, most of it at $8,388.08 every two weeks from late August, including a $5,000 bonus in July. MIR Services started on 4 October and H.I. Immigration paid three small website invoices. Revenue on the T2 was $238,877.

The biggest cost was Zeeshan, $78,680 in sub-contracts. Total expenses were $110,825 and income before tax $128,052. Tax came to $15,848, paid on 5 March 2025; no instalments were paid during 2024.

The first HST year closed on 14 August 2024 and its $23,187.60 balance was paid on 9 October. Two prepayments in December ($11,682) went toward the next HST year.

The T2 2024, signed 19 February 2025 and assessed 28 February, ended with $57,377 of retained earnings and $77,740 in the bank.

Personal

Umar received $100,000 of transfers recorded as dividends; the T2 2024 declares $72,550 of non eligible dividends. The $27,450 gap is an open question for Saira. No 2024 personal return is in the folder.
