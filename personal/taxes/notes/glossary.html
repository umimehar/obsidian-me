BN
Business number: the corporation's nine digit CRA identifier, 795920958.
CCA
Capital cost allowance: deducting the cost of equipment over several years instead of at once.
CCPC
Canadian controlled private corporation. It gets a lower tax rate on its first $500,000 of business income.
CPP
Canada Pension Plan. Employer and employee each pay a share on wages.
CRA
Canada Revenue Agency, the federal tax authority.
director funded expenses
Business costs Umar paid personally that the corporation owes him back. Repaying them is not income to him.
EI
Employment insurance. The owner's spouse is exempt, so Maham's pay has none.
ETA
Excise Tax Act, the law that sets out HST.
FHSA
First home savings account. Contributions reduce taxable income and withdrawals for a first home are tax free.
GIFI
The standard line numbers CRA uses for the income statement and balance sheet in a T2, such as 8000 for revenue.
home office
The part of the home used for the business. The corporation claims 30% of the rent.
HST
Harmonized sales tax: 13% in Ontario. The corporation adds it to invoices and sends the net amount to CRA once a year.
instalments
Advance payments toward tax that is not yet due, so there is little or nothing left to pay when the return is filed.
ITC
Input tax credit: HST the corporation paid on business purchases, which it subtracts from the HST it collected.
NETFILE
CRA's online system for sending a return directly.
notice of assessment
CRA's letter confirming what it accepted from a return and what is owed or refunded.
NR4
A slip for payments to non residents of Canada. Not needed for Zeeshan because he works outside Canada.
PD7A
CRA's statement of payroll remittances made under the RP account.
Reg. 105
The rule that makes payers withhold 15% from non residents for services done in Canada. Does not apply to Zeeshan.
Represent a Client
CRA's online service where a business authorises an accountant to see and file for its accounts.
retained earnings
Profit the corporation has kept over the years after tax and dividends.
RP account
The corporation's payroll account with CRA, 795920958 RP0001, used for remittances.
RRSP
Registered retirement savings plan. Contributions reduce taxable income.
RT account
The corporation's HST account with CRA, 795920958 RT0001.
s.15(2)
The rule in the Income Tax Act that taxes a shareholder loan as income if it is not repaid in time.
s.173
The rule that may add a standby charge when a car the corporation pays for is available to the owner personally.
s.174
The rule that lets a business claim a small HST credit on mileage it pays to someone driving their own car.
s.235
The HST rule that limits the credit on a car lease the same way s.67.3 limits the income tax deduction.
s.67.3
The rule that limits how much of a car lease a business can deduct when the car's list price is high. For this lease 44.93% of each payment counts.
Schedule 1
The part of the T2 that turns accounting profit into taxable income, for example adding back half of meals.
Schedule 100
The balance sheet part of the T2: what the corporation owns and owes at year end.
Schedule 125
The income statement part of the T2: revenue and expenses by GIFI line.
shareholder loan
Money the corporation lends its owner. If not repaid within a year after the year end it becomes the owner's taxable income.
simplified registrant
A foreign seller registered for HST under a simplified system (number ending RT9999); its HST cannot be claimed back as a credit.
small business deduction
The tax cut a CCPC gets on its first $500,000 of income, bringing the Ontario rate to 12.2%.
standby charge
A taxable benefit for having a company car available for personal use (see s.173).
T1
A person's yearly income tax return, due 30 April.
T1135
A form for people holding more than $100,000 of property outside Canada.
T183
The form a taxpayer signs to let an accountant file the return electronically.
T2
The corporation's yearly income tax return, for January to December.
T3
A slip showing income from a trust or fund.
T4
The slip an employer gives an employee showing pay and the tax, CPP and EI taken off it.
T4 Summary
The form filed with CRA alongside the T4 slips, totalling pay, deductions and remittances for the year.
T4A
A slip a payer issues for fees paid to someone who is not an employee.
T4FHSA
The slip showing money put into or taken out of a first home savings account.
T5
A slip showing dividends or interest paid to a person. The corporation files one only in years it pays dividends.
T5008
A slip from a broker listing investments sold during the year, used to work out gains or losses.
TFSA
Tax free savings account. No deduction going in, no tax coming out.
