## What changed

- **2026 costs $10,150 more but deducts $6,220 less.** Two reasons: the full year of lease and insurance, and the 2026 rules (80% business use plus the s.67.3 cap).
- **The lease cap is the big one.** Only 44.93% of the business share of each $1,750.01 payment is deductible. On 2026's $21,000.12 of payments, $9,251.81 is over the cap and $4,200.02 is the personal 20%, leaving $7,548.28.
- **Insurance more than quadruples** because 2025 only had Oct–Dec of the Mercedes policy, and the Oct 1, 2026 renewal is $804.75/month, up from $700.84.
- **Maintenance falls** because 2025 had the $2,817.77 winter tires and 2026 has one service so far.
- **2026 personal use charged to Umar: $7,119.48**, by offset against what the corporation owes him.
