## CRA monthly remitter rule (for reference)

Source deductions for a given pay period are due to CRA by the **15th day of the month following** the month in which the payment was made. If the 15th falls on a weekend or public holiday, the due date moves to the next business day. (CRA — When to remit due dates.)

All 9 scheduled payments above are **on or before** their respective due dates, with most landing 1–11 days early — comfortable margin for any bank-processing delay.
