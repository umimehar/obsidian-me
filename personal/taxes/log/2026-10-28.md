## What this changes

Nothing in the plan. The agent described how the balance will clear on its own, not whether the objection is worth filing. The objection still matters for two reasons:

- Collection. The balance is due 23 November. A notice of objection on file holds CRA collection on the disputed amount while it is open; without one, a six month wait means the account goes to collections first.
- A backstop. If the amended slip is processed but CRA does not reassess the T1 on its own, the objection is the filed record that forces it. The deadline is 20 January 2027, well before the April estimate.

Decided not to ask Lazer to change the recipient name. The BN on the slip was what was needed.

Arrears interest keeps running on the unpaid balance either way and reverses with the T4A part when the reassessment is corrected.
