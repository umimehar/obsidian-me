## Qualification status

The gates that disqualify a bid on the compliance check, before anyone reads the approach. Fill these in once and reuse them on every proposal.

| Requirement | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Three comparable public sector references | ☐ | The chicken and egg problem. Subcontract under a prime first. |
| Commercial general liability, $2M to $5M | ☐ | Certificate of insurance, named buyer as additional insured |
| Errors and omissions / professional liability | ☐ | Commonly $1M to $2M for digital work |
| WCB or CNESST clearance letter | ☐ | Province of your registered office |
| WCAG 2.1 AA delivery evidence | ☐ | Named project plus audit method, not a claim |
| Canadian data residency hosting story | ☐ | Named region, named provider, in writing |
| Business registration and, where asked, a bid bond | ☐ | |
| BC Bid / APC / MERX / bids&tenders / Biddingo accounts | ☐ | Register once, set saved search alerts |
| SAP Ariba registration for federal | ☐ | Prerequisite for federal bidding |
| TBIPS / SBIPS supply arrangement | ☐ | Open until 2028-07-04. The only realistic federal route. |
