# 2026-08-10 — Endeavor created, market scanned, digest built

## What the scan found

Started from a CanadaBuys search filtered to open service contracts matching "website": 242 results. Extracted 200 of them and checked every title. **Ten are about a website.** The search is full text, so the other 232 matched phrases like "documents are available on our website" and are winter road maintenance, window cleaning, trip hazard removal and mosquito viral testing contracts.

Of the ten, seven had not closed. Three still showed status Open with closing dates in 2024 and 2025, so the partner feed goes stale and nobody prunes it. All ten are municipal, academic or provincial. Zero federal.

The federal picture came from the open data export rather than the search, since the export is complete and never rate limited: **893 open notices, three touching web work.** A CRA secure online portal, an NRC content management system posted as an advance contract award notice (winner already chosen), and an EDC user experience research contract. Federal web work flows through the TBIPS and SBIPS supply arrangements instead, both open for qualification until 2028-07-04.

Opened the BC Pension notice to check the bidding path. CanadaBuys mirrors the notice and links out to `bcbid.gov.bc.ca`; the RFP and appendices live there. **You cannot submit through CanadaBuys for anything non federal.** Scope was also larger than the title suggested: twelve web properties, six brands, off Liferay, design system plus managed hosting plus Canadian data residency, thirty six months.

## What got built

`tender-digest` skill in the `dev` vault at `config/claude/skills/tender-digest/`. Two feeds, four email sections, 30 tests, no model in the loop.

The selection rule is the product: **match on the title, not the body.** That took precision from 4% to 100% on the sample above.

Scheduled Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 08:12 local via `com.tender-digest.scan`, matching the three `com.news-digest.*` jobs already installed.

## Findings worth keeping

- **CanadaBuys 403s a burst.** Five rapid requests tripped the WAF and locked out both curl and WebFetch for several minutes. Paced single requests pass. The fetcher spaces requests seven seconds apart and takes one page per term; results are relevance ranked, so the genuine matches are on page one anyway.
- **Going direct to the provinces buys nothing.** Alberta's `purchasing.alberta.ca/api/opportunity/search` returns 500 on every filter shape tried, and BC Bid publishes no dataset. CanadaBuys already mirrors BC Bid, Alberta Purchasing Connection, MERX and Ontario, so one source covers the country.
- **The two feeds identify notices differently.** The CSV keys on the solicitation number, the search on a URL slug, so the same federal notice arrives twice under different ids. Deduplicating on title plus organisation fixes it; deduplicating on id does not.
- **The classification net needs to stay narrow.** A first pass matching `computer services` and `user interface` dragged in an Oracle ERP functional analyst, a generic software developer requisition and an IT equipment installation contract. Narrowed to `internet or intranet`, `web site` and `content management software`, which still catches TBIPS and SBIPS.
- **Standing offers need their own bucket.** TBIPS, SBIPS, ProServices and the BC Service Delivery lists close hundreds of days out. Sorted by urgency they sink to the bottom and read as irrelevant, when they are the highest leverage items in the list. Anything closing more than 120 days out is now "qualify once".
