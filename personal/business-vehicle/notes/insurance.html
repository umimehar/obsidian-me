What a replacement quote has to carry

Anything missing from this list is a downgrade, whatever the premium says.

  • $2M third party liability
  • DCPD at $0 deductible, or a priced comparison at $500 and $1,000
  • Collision and comprehensive at $1,000
  • All Perils and Specified Perils included
  • Five year new vehicle replacement, not OPCF 43R
  • OPCF 5 naming Mercedes-Benz Financial Services
  • OPCF 20 at $1,500
  • OPCF 27 at $100,000
  • OPCF 35 roadside
  • OPCF 44R at $2M
  • OPCF 47R optional accident benefits

Answered

Asked of MBFS

What is the maximum deductible Mercedes-Benz Financial permits under the lease?

The lease requires a maximum deductible of $2,500 or 5% of the price of the vehicle, whichever is greater, so about $5,223 on the $104,459.95 leased vehicle amount. Minimum third party liability is $1,000,000 per occurrence, with MBFS named as Additional Named Insured and Loss Payee. The insurer must give MBFS 15 days written notice of any change or cancellation. Source: the lease, section 12.
