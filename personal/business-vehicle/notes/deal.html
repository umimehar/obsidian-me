What was good

The 58% residual is generous, and the market now says so

Three year old GLC 43s ask $54,495 to $59,995 today. The guaranteed buyout is $58,580, at the top of that range. Mercedes-Benz Financial carries that depreciation risk, not the lessee.

4.49% is consistent for an AMG variant

A base 2026 GLC leases in Canada at 3.99% as of August 2026, and AMG models carry a premium with less subvention. No published Canadian residual and rate sheet for the AMG GLC 43 as of September 2025 could be found, so this is reasonable rather than verified.

Gap protection came free inside the lease

Section 13 provides it as standard. Many buyers pay a dealer separately for the same thing.

What was not

A 1.89% discount

$2,076 off a $109,929.95 list. This is the weakest number in the file.

$5,107 of soft products and dealer fees

$5,107.00

A $695 administration fee, a $499 window tint, $170 wheel locks, and a prepaid maintenance plan at $1,779 which is exactly $480 plus $801 plus $498, the sum of its three retail services. No discount at all. It bought a fixed price, not a saving.

A kilometre tier above the car's actual use

18,000 a year bought against 15,871 driven. About 6,473 kilometres will go unused, and unused kilometres are never credited back.

Next time

  1. Negotiate the discount before anything else. It is the only number here worth thousands.
  2. Take the 15,000 kilometre tier, not 18,000.
  3. Decline the finance and insurance table, or price each product against buying it later.
  4. Ask what the rate would be on the outgoing model year, which carries more subvention.
