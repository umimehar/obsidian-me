The lease is signed on the basis of primarily business use and the insurance is rated personal, with business kilometres blank on both certificates. One of the two is wrong. If the insurer's version is the wrong one, a claim can be denied outright. Settle this before the renewal binds.

Where this bites

The declared use conflict, everywhere it shows up.

The lease declares business use, the insurance is rated personal

high

Page 7 of the lease carries a signed corporate declaration: the vehicle will be used primarily for business or commercial purposes. The preauthorized payment authorisation on the same page is ticked business purposes. Both insurance certificates rate the car as personal use, with the business kilometre field blank and the payment schedule marked Personal.

Why it matters. An insurer that finds the declared use wrong can deny a claim on the whole policy, not just adjust the premium. This is the largest uninsured exposure on the file and it costs nothing to fix.

Do this. Settle the real business use percentage, then correct the rating with Desjardins or disclose it to the brokers before anyone binds.

Platinum Security may already be void for the same reason

high

Section 3.4 of the Platinum AutoPlus agreement makes a vehicle used for commercial or business purposes an Ineligible Vehicle, with no entitlement to any service. The lease declares exactly that use.

Why it matters. $349 was paid for theft compensation worth up to $7,000 that may not be payable. The 30 day cancellation window closed on 30 October 2025, so the only move left is to establish in writing whether the agreement stands.

Do this. Put the question to First Canadian Protection Products at 1-800-381-2580 in writing, quoting agreement AP-12876238.
