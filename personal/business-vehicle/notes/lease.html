Gap protection is already in the lease

Which changes what a replacement quote has to do.

Built into the lease. When the insurer pays out on a total loss the lease terminates and the proceeds clear the balance. The only amounts still owed are the deductible, any arrears, amounts owing from a breach, and prorated excess kilometre charges.

The condition. MBFS must be consulted before agreeing to any actual cash value settlement. Agreeing without consulting them forfeits gap protection and puts the lease in default.

Excluded: The charging station price, any optional products, and any balance rolled in from a prior lease.

Returning the car

Maturity is 30 September 2028.

Beyond anything unpaid, the charges on return are the cost of repairing excess wear and tear whether or not MBFS actually repairs it, excess kilometres, termination fees and taxes, and $250.00 if the maintenance booklets are missing.

Excess wear and tear counts Overdue service or maintenance; Driveline damage; Dents, scratches, cracks, rips, holes, tears, leaks or rust; Frame damage; Missing parts or accessories; Missing tyres, tyres of lesser quality than original, or tyres with less than 3.2 mm tread remaining; Water damage; Electrical or mechanical parts not working; Broken or burned out lights, chipped or cracked glass, any glass the lessee tinted; Damage making the vehicle unsafe or unlawful; Any damage insurance would cover, whether or not insurance is in force.

Overholding. Double the monthly payment for each of the first two months, triple every month after
