## RRSP contributions were undercounting by $8,000

Reported 2026 RRSP room used was $25,000 against a stated actual of roughly $32k. Traced the gap: on 2026-05-06 the hub account sent $8,000 described as "Transfer out to RRSP", and it landed in the `PE` account (`WK63GZF41`) as a `CONT` contribution. `detectKind` mapped `PE-` filenames to their own kind, so the account never reached the RRSP room bar.

## RRSP room now comes from the notice of assessment

The room bar was measuring against `CONTRIBUTION_LIMITS`, the generic CRA annual maximum ($33,810 for 2026). That ignores carry-forward, so it read as nearly full when there is plenty of room.

Added `ASSESSED_ROOM` in `analytics.ts` for personal room transcribed from an NOA, emitted as `assessed_room` on the ledger. `taxSummary` prefers it over the annual maximum and sets `assessed: true` on the row; `sections.ts` labels such a bar "assessed" and swaps the footnote, since the carry-forward caveat does not apply to it.

2025 NOA gives 2026 RRSP room of $70,752 (45,191 unused at the end of 2025 plus 25,561 earned in 2025). Against $33,000 contributed, $37,752 remains. Written up in [[rrsp-room]].

Five tests added: assessed room overriding the maximum, no leak into an adjacent year, and the `roomLimit` fallbacks. 117 pass.

## Filter scope now syncs to the query string

New `src/client/url.ts` owns the contract: `accts` (short_ids), `t`, `from`/`to`, `period`. Every param is omitted at its default, so a default view has a bare URL. Writes use `replaceState`, so Back still leaves the page in one press.

Checked the constraint that decided the design rather than assuming it: `history.replaceState` with a query string is permitted on a `file://` document in Chrome, so the query string works even though the page is opened from the vault as a local file. The call is still wrapped, so a stricter browser loses linkability and nothing else.

`decodeScope` is total, since a hand-edited URL still has to open. Verified in headless Chrome against the real built page: two accounts plus a preset restore correctly; a custom range plus `period=2026-05` reopens the May drill-down; a stale period is dropped and scrubbed; all-garbage params yield a clean default view.

16 tests added. Mutation-checked four of them (inverted range, empty-list guard, period validation, comma unescaping) — each mutation fails the suite, so they are not vacuous. 133 pass overall.
