# 2026-09-27

TCK-0008, the last dashboard v2 ticket. Holdings tab and the XEQT benchmark on Growth. 1639 tests, six commits across an implementation pass and a review round.

## Holdings combined across accounts

`analytics/holdings.ts` filters every counted account to its BROKERAGE statement at the single latest period any counted account reports, combined down to one row per symbol and price currency, plus a cash row per currency. An account with no statement at that exact period contributes nothing and is named in `behind` on the tab rather than silently dropped. It reconciles to the portfolio total within a cent or two per account; that residual is stated with its sign, not just its size.

A row is keyed by symbol AND its resolved price currency together, never by symbol alone: a bare ticker can name two unrelated companies on different exchanges. This corpus's own example is "L", Loblaw Cos. Ltd. in CAD across the FHSA and one non registered account, and Loews Corp. in USD in another; keying by symbol alone merged the two into one invented $9,514.36 holding in the first pass, caught in review.

The $0 price and CAD default quirk this project has hit before (a statement that omits a holding's price parses as CAD, marketPrice 0) is corrected by looking up other properly priced sightings of the same symbol, preferring the same account before the same period, so the same ticker collision cannot pick the wrong company's currency.

S&P 500 exposure (VFV, VOO and the rest of `INDEX_GROUPS`) only lists the symbols actually held, with the accounts holding them read off the model rather than a second table. WSE401, the one pending valuation holding, is badged on the page rather than left to look like any other row.

## Benchmark: the same deposits in XEQT

`bun run benchmark` fetches XEQT.TO's daily closes from Yahoo (browser user agent, `query2` not `query1`, which answered where `query1` returned 429 from this machine), keeps each month's last trading day's adjusted close so distributions read as reinvested, and drops the current, still open calendar month so a fetch taken mid month can never be mistaken for a real month end close. The corpus starts 2023-06 and XEQT has traded since 2019.

`simulateBenchmark` replays the portfolio's own net deposits (the same basis `netFlowsByPeriod` feeds the fitted rate and the return chart with) as unit purchases in XEQT at that month's close, opening with the portfolio's own starting market value, never book cost. A month with no close is left out and named in the chart's own note, never zero filled or carried forward.

The benchmark chart sits first on Growth, ahead of the returns grid drawn one line per account, since a single number against a single fund is the more direct answer to "how well is it doing." The chart states its result visibly above the lines, not only in the aria-label, and the disclosure names two real limitations rather than glossing over them: buying at each month's closing price favours the portfolio somewhat in a rising market, and deposits made in USD cash are not yet counted as deposits at all, a known gap tracked separately.

## Review round

A first review caught the symbol collision above, asked for goldens pinning holdings and benchmark figures rather than only fixture tests, asked for the visible chart summary, and caught a partial current month landing in the committed benchmark data. All fixed, each with its own commit and a mutation proof.

## Deviation worth recording

The Holdings tab does not recompute holdings for a selected year. Holdings breakdown by symbol only exists at the build step, from raw statement data the browser bundle never receives (unlike account level market value, which is already carried forward per month in `analytics.json`). Building a full per year holdings history was out of scope for this ticket; the tab states plainly that the year filter does not change what it shows.
