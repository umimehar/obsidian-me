# 2026-09-27

TCK-0008, the last dashboard v2 ticket. Holdings tab and the XEQT benchmark on Growth. 1609 tests, three commits.

## Holdings combined across accounts

`analytics/holdings.ts` combines every counted account's own latest BROKERAGE statement down to one row per symbol, plus a cash row per currency. It reconciles to the portfolio total within a cent or two per account, since an account whose latest statement is CASH only contributes nothing here even though it counts toward the total. That residual is stated on the tab rather than hidden.

The $0 price and CAD default quirk this project has hit before (a statement that omits a holding's price parses as CAD, marketPrice 0) is corrected by looking up any other statement that priced the same symbol.

S&P 500 exposure (VFV, VOO and the rest of `INDEX_GROUPS`) only lists the symbols actually held, with the accounts holding them read off the model rather than a second table.

## Benchmark: the same deposits in XEQT

`bun run benchmark` fetches XEQT.TO's daily closes from Yahoo (browser user agent, `query2` not `query1`, which answered where `query1` returned 429 from this machine) and keeps each month's last trading day's adjusted close, so distributions read as reinvested. The corpus starts 2023-06 and XEQT has traded since 2019, so every month is comparable.

`simulateBenchmark` replays the portfolio's own net deposits (the same basis `netFlowsByPeriod` feeds the fitted rate and the return chart with) as unit purchases in XEQT at that month's close, opening with the portfolio's own starting value. A month with no close is left out and named in the chart's own note, never zero filled or carried forward.

The benchmark chart sits first on Growth, ahead of the per-account returns grid, since a single number against a single fund is the more direct answer to "how well is it doing" than fifteen account lines.

## Deviation worth recording

The Holdings tab does not recompute holdings for a selected year. Holdings breakdown by symbol only exists at the build step, from raw statement data the browser bundle never receives (unlike account level market value, which is already carried forward per month in `analytics.json`). Building a full per year holdings history was out of scope for this ticket; the tab states plainly that the year filter does not change what it shows.
