# Allocation and RRSP plan, October 2026

Decided 2026-10-01. The structured form of everything below is `strategy` in `data/plan.json`, which the Future tab renders as the Plan panel. This note carries the reasoning.

## Direct indexing: 70% U.S. Market Index, 30% U.S. Innovation Index

Direct indexing is Non-registered 1f9a, $35,583 at 2026-10-01, all of it in the U.S. Market Index. The owner first set 60% Market, 30% Innovation, 5% AAPL and 5% NVDA, and changed it to 70/30 the same day. Why the single stock sleeves were dropped:

- AAPL and NVDA were already the two largest positions inside the Market sleeve, at 7.26% and 7.95% in August. With their Innovation weight and a 5% direct sleeve each, both land near 12 to 13% of the account. Those figures assume Nasdaq 100 style weights for the Innovation Index; Wealthsimple's methodology was not checked.
- In Canada identical shares form one ACB pool across sleeves and accounts, and the superficial loss rule reaches the TFSA, the RRSP, the spouse and the two corporations. A stock held in two sleeves makes loss harvesting on it messy.
- The tilt already existed before this change: about $41,800, 17% of the portfolio, across QQC, HXQ, HXQ.U, CHPS, CHPX, VGT and VOOG. The 70/30 split takes it to about 21%.

The split is filled by deposits only. A deposit in Automated Investing buys the underweight holding first and never sells, while Rebalance now would sell about 30% of the Market sleeve, which carried a $4,641 unrealized gain. Filling the Innovation sleeve to 30% takes about $15,250 of deposits at today's values.

Direct indexing takes CAD deposits and converts at Wealthsimple's corporate rate with a spread of 0% to 0.05%, so the 1.5% conversion fee of the self directed USD account does not apply. The account's statement fee ran about 0.28% a year in August.
